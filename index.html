02 — The coverApproval gate

Anything risky ships with a cover on it.

Shutdowns, deletes, shell commands. Each arrives as a card that says exactly what will run, and waits for your thumb. Nothing happens in between.

  • COnly you. Paired to your account. Guests get an allow-list and a folder they can't leave.
